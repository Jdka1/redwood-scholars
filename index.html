I.

Who We Are

We help students and their families with college applications. We’re Aryan and Izzy, two Stanford students, and we work directly with your son or daughter from the first conversation through submission. There is no associate or junior coach standing in for us.

We are not career coaches. We went through the application process ourselves not long ago, and we remember how hard it was to find guidance that felt honest. We take on a small number of students at a time so that we can give each one real attention.

III.

How the Process Works

Every family gets the same clear path, so you always know what we are working on, what comes next, and what is expected of the student.

  1. 1

    A free intro conversation

    You and your student tell us where things stand, which schools are on your mind, and what you hope to get from working together. We answer your questions and tell you honestly whether we are a good fit.

  2. 2

    A written plan in the first few weeks

    We review the student’s grades, activities, and interests, then give you a written summary, a realistic school list, and a timeline with dates for essays, tests, and deadlines. You see it before we begin regular work.

  3. 3

    Regular one-on-one sessions

    We meet with the student weekly or every other week, with session length to fit the student’s needs, to brainstorm essays, shape their activities list, and work through each application. Students bring drafts, and we give specific written feedback on each one.

  4. 4

    Clear updates for parents

    After sessions we send a short note on what we covered and what the student is working on next. Parents are welcome to ask questions along the way.

  5. 5

    A final review before anything is submitted

    We go through each full application with the student to check that it is complete, accurate, and ready. The student always makes the final decisions and submits their own application.

The student’s voice stays their own. We guide and give feedback, and we never write essays for students.

IV.

What You Can Expect

  • You work directly with us. No associates handle your student's file.
  • You get a written plan in the first few weeks, so you know what we are working toward and why.
  • You get specific, honest feedback on every draft.
  • We keep our roster small, so each student gets our full attention.
V.

What Is Expected of the Student

We give every student plenty of hands-on support, and we want the process to be a great experience for them. We also push each student to be their best and reach the best outcome they can. In return, we ask that they:

  • Write their own essays, from the first draft to the last.
  • Come to sessions prepared, having done the reading or thinking we agreed on.
  • Be honest with us about their grades, their time, and what they actually want, even when it is inconvenient to say.

We are here to guide and support each student, celebrate their voice, and help them grow into the strongest applicant they can be.

VI.

Communication With You

You are paying for this, and you deserve to know what is happening throughout. We send you a short written update after each stage of the process, and we are glad to get on a call whenever you would like one.

We do not keep the student's progress from you. If the student asks us to treat something as private between us, we will tell you that much, and nothing more.
