III.

How the Process Works

  1. 1

    An initial conversation

    You and the student tell us where things stand, what schools are on your mind, and what you are hoping to get out of working together.

  2. 2

    A written plan

    We put together an assessment of the student's record and interests, along with a realistic school list and a rough timeline.

  3. 3

    Regular sessions with the student

    We meet throughout the year to work on essays, activities, and the application itself. We read drafts and respond with direct feedback.

  4. 4

    A final review

    Before anything is submitted, we go through the full application together to make sure it is complete, accurate, and ready.

IV.

What You Can Expect

  • Direct access to us. Not a platform, and not a team of associates working on your student's file.
  • A written plan early on, so you know what we are working toward and why.
  • Honest, specific feedback, rather than reassurance for its own sake.
  • A deliberately small roster, so the student has our real attention rather than a fraction of it.
V.

What Is Expected of the Student

This only works if the student does the work. We ask that they:

  • Write their own essays, from the first draft to the last.
  • Come to sessions prepared, having done the reading or thinking we agreed on.
  • Be honest with us about their grades, their time, and what they actually want, even when it is inconvenient to say.

We are there to guide the student and to push back when it is warranted, not to do the work for them.

VI.

Communication With You

You are paying for this, and you deserve to know what is happening throughout. We send you a short written update after each stage of the process, and we are glad to get on a call whenever you would like one.

We do not keep the student's progress from you. If the student asks us to treat something as private between us, we will tell you that much, and nothing more.
